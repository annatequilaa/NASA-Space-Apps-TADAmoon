Data & method

The landing planner uses JPL DE421 and the DE421 lunar orientation kernels. The orbit simulator uses DE440. The browser interpolates tabulated vectors. Distances and event times are approximate.

The terrain view uses the supplied 40 × 40 elevation grids and 360-point skyline profiles. Display relief and marker size are illustrative. Reference coordinates use a flat skyline. Nearby custom points can borrow a profile within 10 km.

Clearance compares the Sun’s upper limb or Earth’s center with the local skyline. The planner uses hourly forecast samples, a modeled mast-height correction and an adjustable battery assumption. A −2° floor in the supplied profiles can make a visibility estimate uncertain.
